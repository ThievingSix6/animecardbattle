DESTINATION BUILDINGS
=====================

One optional model per place in the city. The filename is the
destination id:

  card_shop.glb    Card Shop      opens the collection
  summon.glb       Summon Altar   opens summoning
  talents.glb      Talent Shrine  opens talents
  team.glb         War Camp       opens the team builder
  campaign.glb     Campaign Gate  opens the campaign
  clan.glb         Clan Hall      opens the clan
  settings.glb     Settings Shop  opens settings
  tower.glb        The Tower      Diablo and the Hellfire Gauntlet

Any of these that is missing falls back to props/building.glb, so the
city is complete whether you have one model or all eight.

The tower is built at 46 m and everything else at 14 m; scale and
origin in the file do not matter.
