SCATTER PROPS
=============

The open-ended one. ANY model dropped in here turns up along the
city's kerbs - no registration, no list to maintain, no code change.
The folder is the list.

Ninety props are placed, dealt out between however many models are in
here, pushed to the edge of the nearest street so they line the roads
rather than standing in them. Each is scaled to 3-7 m.

Things that would suit it: vending machines, benches, bus stops, phone
boxes, bollards, bins, crates, market stalls, torii gates, food carts,
parked bikes, road signs, traffic lights, air-con units, barriers.

Scale and origin do not matter. Nothing here has collision - they are
scenery to drive past, not obstacles.

Change SCATTER_COUNT and SCATTER_HEIGHT_MIN / MAX in
scripts/lobby3d/lobby_world.gd to place more or fewer, or bigger ones.


THE STATUE
----------

Separate from this folder, because there is only one and it has a
specific place: res://art/models/props/statue.glb stands in the middle
of the town square, lit from below, with the portal moved aside to
make room. It is scaled to about 38 m tall.

Without it the square builds a lit obelisk instead.
