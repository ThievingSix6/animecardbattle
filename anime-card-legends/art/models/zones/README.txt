ZONE MODELS
===========

One file per campaign zone. The filename must match the zone id
exactly, all lowercase:

  proving.glb     Ashen Proving Grounds
  hollow.glb      Verdant Hollow
  emberfall.glb   Emberfall Reach
  duskmire.glb    Duskmire Bastion
  choir.glb       Choir of Silence
  heart.glb       The Tower's Heart

Each model is reused for all seven stages of its zone and grows from
4.5 m at stage 1 to 16 m at the boss. Scale and origin do not matter -
the game measures the model and rescales it.

Full notes in the README one folder up.
