BANNER ARTWORK
==============

Drop one image here per banner and it becomes that banner's splash on
the Summon screen. Name the file after the banner id:

    standard.png    Eternal Archive
    ember.png       Emberfall Ascendant
    abyss.png       Abyssal Tide
    radiance.png    Radiant Choir
    nightfall.png   Nightmare Court
    bastion.png     Ironroot Bastion
    legends.png     Legends Awakened

.png, .jpg, .jpeg and .webp all work. A wide image reads best - the
panel is roughly 3:1 and crops to fill, so keep the subject centred.

Any banner without an image draws a themed gradient from its accent
colour instead, so the screen is never broken while art is missing.
The banner list itself lives in scripts/core/banners.gd.
